# CivicFix 60-Second Pitch

Civic problems are everywhere—potholes, illegal dumping, broken streetlights. We see them every day, but reporting them is tedious. You have to find the right website, pick the exact category, and write a formal complaint. Because it's hard, most people simply walk away.

CivicFix solves this by letting citizens just show the problem.

*Demonstrate taking a photo of a civic issue*

You snap a photo, and our multimodal AI does the heavy lifting. It identifies the issue, determines its severity, and structures a perfect, actionable report. The citizen just verifies the location and clicks submit. The data hits our dashboard, instantly giving municipalities a bird's-eye view of civic health.

By reducing the friction of reporting to a single photo, we empower citizens and drastically reduce triage time for cities.

CivicFix turns a photo of a problem into the first step toward solving it.
