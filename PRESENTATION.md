# CivicFix Presentation Outline

*(Exactly 5 Slides)*

## Slide 1 — Problem
**Civic problems are visible. Reporting them isn't.**
- *Visuals*: Show examples of everyday issues: garbage dumps, potholes, broken streetlights, blocked drains.
- *Talking point*: Citizens see these daily, but traditional reporting systems are confusing and cause high friction.

## Slide 2 — Solution
**CivicFix**
> Spot it. Report it. Fix it.
- *Visuals*: The core flow visual: `Photo ↓ AI ↓ Structured Report ↓ Track`
- *Talking point*: Instead of making citizens fill out forms, they just take a photo. The AI does the heavy lifting.

## Slide 3 — How It Works
**Architecture & Technology**
- *Visuals*: A minimal flowchart (Next.js → Image Processing → AI Vision → Database → Dashboard).
- *Talking point*: We use client-side compression to handle large files, and robust AI parsing to guarantee the municipality gets perfectly structured JSON data every time.

## Slide 4 — Live Product
**The CivicFix Experience**
- *Visuals*: Clean screenshots of the deployed application (do not include API keys or secrets):
  - The Report flow (AI analyzing)
  - The AI analysis result (Review screen)
  - The Tracking timeline
  - The Community Dashboard
- *Talking point*: The app is mobile-first, heavily polished, and designed to inspire trust.

## Slide 5 — Impact & Future
**From MVP to City-Scale**
- **Current MVP**: AI-assisted reporting, structured data, location tagging, timeline tracking, community dashboard.
- **Future**: 
  - Municipal integrations (311 APIs)
  - Automated department routing
  - Multilingual support
  - Duplicate issue detection
  - City-scale heatmaps and analytics
