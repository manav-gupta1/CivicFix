# CivicFix - Final Hackathon Demo Script

The demo must fit within 90 seconds.

### 0–10 sec: Landing Page
- Open landing page.
- Explain the problem: *"Civic problems are easy to see but hard to report. CivicFix turns everyday civic problems into structured, trackable reports using AI."*

### 10–25 sec: Upload a Civic Issue Image
- Click **Report a Problem**.
- Upload a demonstration image (e.g., Roadside garbage accumulation).
- Emphasize that the citizen only needs to take a photo.

### 25–40 sec: Show AI Classification
- Wait for the AI loading sequence to finish.
- Highlight the AI result:
  - **Category**: Waste
  - **Severity**: High
  - **Confidence**: 94%
  - **Generated Description**: "A significant accumulation of mixed waste..."
- Mention that this standardizes data for the city without burdening the user.

### 40–55 sec: Confirm Location & Submit
- Click **Confirm & Continue**.
- Show the location tagging (e.g., Yamuna Vihar, Delhi).
- Click **Submit Report**.
- Show the smooth submission and the generated `CF-XXXXX` report ID.

### 55–70 sec: Tracking Page
- Open the report tracking page.
- Show the visual timeline (`Submitted → Assigned → In Progress → Resolved`).
- Explain how this builds trust between the citizen and the municipality.

### 70–90 sec: Dashboard
- Open the Dashboard.
- Show the high-level community metrics (e.g., 127 total reports, 65% resolution rate).
- Explain how this dashboard can scale to larger civic workflows, providing real-time infrastructure analytics for city officials.
